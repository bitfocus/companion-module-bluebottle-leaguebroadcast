# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive technical feedback, and keep discussions
focused on improving the module. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these
expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to [lars@bluebottle.gg](mailto:lars@bluebottle.gg). Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
