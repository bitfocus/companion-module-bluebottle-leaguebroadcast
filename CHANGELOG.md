# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-25

- Initial release.
- Control LeagueBroadcast over its authenticated RPC API: in-game overlay toggles, caster pages,
  recaps, post-game stat screens, cinematics, rehearsal (mock) data, series and game-winner
  control, and a panic hide-all button.
- Zero-configuration connection on the same machine, mDNS discovery on the local network, and
  pairing-token authenticated control from a remote Companion host.
- Variables, feedbacks, and presets covering connection state, game phase, team and series
  metadata, active overlays and pages, hotkey state, and tier entitlement.
